Financial Education Learning Modules

Core financial literacy guides explaining budgeting, compounding, and investment risks.

1. The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

50% Needs (Rent, Food), 30% Wants (Entertainment, Dining), 20% Savings & Emergency Fund.

2. Compound Interest & Rule of 72

Divide 72 by annual interest rate to find years required to double your capital (72/8 = 9 years).

3. Inflation & Purchasing Power

6% annual inflation halves purchasing power in 12 years. Idle cash in a 3% account loses real buying value.

4. Credit Cards & Instant Loan Traps

Unpaid credit card balances compound at 36%-42% APR. Always pay total bill, not minimum due.
